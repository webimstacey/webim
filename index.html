About WeBIM

Where digital information meets commercial intelligence.

WeBIM Consultancy Pte. Ltd. is built around a simple idea: better project information leads to better commercial decisions.

We connect the model, the measurement and the money.

Our approach brings BIM and Quantity Surveying closer together, helping project teams understand scope, quantities, costs and construction implications earlier and with greater confidence.

  • Clearer quantities from coordinated digital models
  • Earlier visibility of design and cost changes
  • Structured information for procurement and tendering
  • Commercial support throughout the project lifecycle
Our Services

One consultancy.
Multiple project needs.

From design development to final account, WeBIM provides integrated technical and commercial support.

01 / BIM

BIM Modelling

Architectural, structural and MEP modelling with project information structured for coordination and delivery.

02 / BIM

BIM Coordination

Model review, clash detection and interdisciplinary coordination to reduce construction uncertainty.

5D & Digital Construction
